00   /   BEFORE YOU START

Three things that
often get mixed up.

01 / YOUR APP

The provider OAuth app

Your integration's registration: client ID, redirect URL, and requested permissions. It identifies your product to the provider.

02 / EACH CUSTOMER

The user's connection

Each user or workspace still consents to connect. Your integration handles that authorization and safeguards the resulting tokens.

03 / MAYBE A GATE

Provider review

Verification or directory review is a separate checkpoint—not the OAuth flow itself. A managed-auth platform may own the provider app; check before registering your own.
