# Limitations

- The built-in library contains four examples, not 100 supported app guides.
- The live research flow uses public search results and excerpts to assemble five structured results. It is not an LLM, does not read or verify every provider page end-to-end, and can return incomplete, stale, or off-topic sources. A domain or app-name match is only a relevance signal, not proof that the source is authoritative.
- The generated actions are a generic OAuth checklist. They may not describe the exact provider flow, requirements, or approval path. Open the provider's official documentation and verify all requirements directly.
- The live generator does not determine reliable provider-specific setup time, review duration, eligibility, or approval outcome. Treat its generic setup baseline and all other planning estimates accordingly; unknown approval requirements or waits remain unknown.
- No provider app was created, no credentials were issued, and no consent or provider approval flow was submitted as part of this prototype.
- Generated guides are not stored by the server. Sharing a generated result reruns the searches, so sources and output may change.
- A production deployment, public source repository, persistent research history, and a complete security or privacy review are outside this prototype's scope.
